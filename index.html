Getting started

Open the Branches tab and add the places you visit. On Today, choose one and tap Check In. Use Notes and Checklist during the visit, then Check Out. Replay the tour any time in Settings → Show the tour.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud. Check Settings → Data → iCloud backup.

The check-out reminder doesn't appear. Turn it on in Settings, allow notifications, and set Location to “Always” for Chronotope in iPhone Settings.

How do I switch to Arabic? Settings → Language, then choose العربية.

How do I create a report? Analysis → share button → PDF Report or Excel.
